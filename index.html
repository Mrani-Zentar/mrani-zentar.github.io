O. Mrani-Zentar

PhD researcher in stochastic control and learning

I work on the mathematical theory of decision-making under uncertainty, with a focus on decentralized stochastic control, data-driven control, and reinforcement learning. My doctoral research contributes to the theory of learning and optimization in complex environments.

Email Google Scholar arXiv

Research Interests

  • Stochastic Control Robustness and learning in complex systems.
  • Machine Learning Reinforcement learning, data-driven control and learning, adaptive control.
  • Decentralized stochastic control Multi-agent problems with a focus on challenges related to partial information and information structures.
  • Probability Theory and Applications Kernel topologies, non-linear filter stability in multi-agent systems.
